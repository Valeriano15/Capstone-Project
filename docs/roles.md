Everyone contributes to the literature review, testing, report writing, and the presentation.
No member should be limited to formatting the paper.

## Weekly working pattern

- One short progress meeting.
- Demonstrate a working increment, not just a description of planned work.
- Record completed work, blockers, decisions, evidence, and next milestones.
- Review changes before merging into the shared branch.
- Update the paper and notes while the work is fresh.
- Track individual contributions accurately for the contribution record.

Suggested task-board states: Backlog, Ready, In progress, Review, Done.

## Presentation split

Each member presents for 4 to 6 minutes. A natural division:

1. Problem, research question, scope, and the authorized lab.
2. Architecture and the human-approval boundary.
3. Discovery, validation, evidence, and the AI reporting safeguards.
4. Experimental conditions, results, limitations, and team contributions.

The live demo of the AI component is graded, so rehearse it and keep a clearly labeled recorded
backup in case the live run fails.
